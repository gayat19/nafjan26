 Basic – SELECT / WHERE

1. List all book titles from the `titles` table.

2. Show only `title` and `price` from `titles`.

3. Find all books where price is greater than 15.

4. Display books whose `type` is `'business'`.

5. Show all authors who live in `'CA'` (California).

---

 ORDER BY

6. List all titles sorted by `price` ascending.

7. Show titles sorted by `price` descending.

8. Display authors sorted by `last name` A–Z.

---

 OFFSET / FETCH (Pagination)

9. Show first 5 titles ordered by price.

10. Skip first 5 titles and show next 5 titles ordered by price.

11. Skip first 10 authors and show next 3 authors ordered by last name.

---

 GROUP BY

12. Count how many books exist for each `type`.

13. Find average price of books by `type`.

14. Show total advance amount grouped by `type`.

15. Count number of authors per `state`.

---

 HAVING

16. Show book types that have more than 3 books.

17. Show states having more than 2 authors.

18. Display book types where average price is greater than 20.

---

 Mixed Practice

19. Show top 5 most expensive books using `ORDER BY` + `OFFSET/FETCH`.

20. List book types with total price greater than 100, sorted by total price descending.
